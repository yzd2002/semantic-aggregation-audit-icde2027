# Reading the evaluation

The paper follows one measured Sports COUNT query through checking, complete execution, and the full policy bill. Section II separates output delivery, fidelity to independent singleton runs S1/S2, and accuracy against supplied source labels. Section III explains each algorithmic step before the probability bound. Section IV distinguishes a workload, a configuration, a probe budget, and a complete policy execution.

S1/S2 are evaluation references. Checking obtains its own singleton responses, and fallback obtains new complete singleton outputs. Consequently, an S1 COUNT in the prose and a checked fallback COUNT in Table III need not be equal. Likewise, the sampled checking/validation mismatches and the full deployment disagreements refer to different stages.

Section VI considers a separate task: auditing or estimating COUNT with access to target labels. The two error intervals count rows, not percentages; their sum/difference distinction explains why wrong row labels may cancel in COUNT. The supplied-label results set the target vector to the dataset labels.
