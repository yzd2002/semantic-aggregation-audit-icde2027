# Post-hoc reference-composition analysis of 96 WANDS pools

After observing all WANDS samples and the overall 96-query outcomes, inspect whether the trace-derived pointwise-disagreement fallback's previously reported ratio-MAE difference from the original keyed plan is concentrated in pools with no Exact-positive judgments. The source, queried labels, original outputs and sample were already exposed. This is not an independent dataset, preregistration, causal analysis or an online routing rule; group membership uses the reference labels unavailable before inference.

For the recorded 96-query sample, reconstruct original and stable-reversed keyed vectors and all pointwise labels from saved final responses with the original strict output grammar. Verify query, local ID, chunk position, presentation order and Exact=Y mapping. Derive the pointwise-fallback and lexical-fallback vectors from those saved traces; the 96-query fallback was virtual and was not measured as a selective scheduler. Verify the reconstructed pointwise-fallback and lexical vectors against independently saved reference predictions. Recompute the original, fallback, lexical-fallback and all-pointwise quality on all 96 pools and match the previously saved primary and secondary ratio-MAE differences, original MAEs and 382 routed candidate disagreements.

Partition all queries into all-negative, all-positive or mixed according to their existing gold Y/N labels. For each nonempty group report query count, keyed ratio MAE, trace-derived fallback ratio MAE, their mean paired difference, its exact query-weighted contribution to the whole-sample difference, and the number/fraction of routed candidate disagreements. Also report lexical-fallback and all-pointwise ratio-MAE descriptives for context; they are not the primary effect or separate confirmatory tests. Use 4,000 within-group paired query bootstrap resamples for the fallback-minus-keyed difference, seed 20271224 + group-index where the fixed group order is all_negative, mixed, all_positive. Report point estimates and 2.5/97.5 percentiles regardless of direction or overlap with zero. Assert that contributions reconstruct the saved whole-sample difference and that group sizes and route counts sum to their totals.

The intervals are exploratory and unadjusted for multiple comparisons; repeated product IDs, source selection, pretraining exposure and proxy relevance labels remain limitations. Small or absent groups should not be treated as population evidence. This decomposition neither proves that composition causes any effect nor estimates a strategy selectable before seeing labels. No new model call, measurement of selective latency or annotation is performed.
