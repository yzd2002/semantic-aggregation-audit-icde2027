# Post-result figure annotation improvement

After the 7B results became available, augment the pre-outcome nine-contrast design from PROTOCOL_7b_figure.md by displaying each model's original and selective absolute ratio MAEs beside its interval and pool count. This avoids suggesting that a larger negative change implies better absolute quality: for example, a model may improve relative to its own keyed baseline while remaining worse than another model or a simple reference. Do not omit or alter any planned dataset, model, interval, failure or result; this is a post-result presentation change, not a pre-registered display or evidence of causality. The user-facing caption must state that values are model-specific common-complete pools and that query-bootstrap intervals are descriptive and uncorrected; models, input templates and scheduling differ.
