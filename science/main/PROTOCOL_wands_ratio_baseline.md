# Post-hoc WANDS ratio-oriented lexical references

This analysis is proposed after observing the test samples, Qwen outputs, lexical and embedding comparisons and the existing cross-validated F1 threshold. It is not a blind evaluation or new model inference. Its purpose is to ask whether the supervised lexical reference is better matched to query-ratio MAE when its decision rule uses the same metric, and to report adverse outcomes without tuning on test labels. The classifier has 5,120 extra training labels; zero-shot Qwen and all-query-words do not.

Use the saved 160-query training inputs and the existing five-fold, query/product/text-disjoint out-of-fold probabilities. Choose a single score threshold minimizing the **query-macro mean absolute ratio error** of those out-of-fold predictions on the training labels. Candidate thresholds are each distinct out-of-fold score and an all-negative threshold immediately above the largest score; ties in objective select the largest threshold. Training-fold probabilities come from models fitted without the respective held-out query, sampled product IDs or exact serialized product texts. The selected out-of-fold score is used for threshold selection and is not an independent estimate of performance. Apply the selected threshold, without test-label tuning, to the already-saved probabilities of the model fitted on all training data.

Also compute two ratio-only references: the unthresholded mean of the saved candidate scores and a constant equal to the lower median of the 160 training-query positive fractions. Neither provides candidate labels. Do not call the unthresholded scores calibrated probabilities or assign candidate F1 to either ratio-only reference. The empirical training-label fractions, classifier and scores are all from the same dataset; uncertainty about threshold selection is not included in test intervals.

Evaluate all three previously exposed WANDS samples: the 160-query keyed holdout, 96-query saved-output fallback, and 52-query actual selective plan. Use the same sampled candidates, label mapping, common completed pools and saved Qwen/lexical predictions. Verify matching query IDs and labels, test/training disjointness for normalized query strings, sampled product IDs and exact product text, and reconstruct or cross-check the recorded Qwen and 0.5-threshold metrics. Report ratio MAE for all methods, candidate F1 and balanced accuracy only where binary vectors exist, and paired 4,000-draw query-bootstrap percentile intervals for ratio-threshold minus Qwen and mean-score minus Qwen using seeds 20261031, 20261032 and 20261033. These comparisons are post-hoc, unadjusted, conditional on the sampled judged pools and vulnerable to cross-query product reuse; no causal, population-coverage or deployment claim is justified. Retain input hashes, score and threshold definitions, tie handling, both favorable and unfavorable outcomes, and scripts. Independently check the selected threshold against a direct sweep over all candidates.
