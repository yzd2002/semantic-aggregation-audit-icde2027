# Post-hoc cross-validated threshold for the WANDS supervised lexical reference

This reference is specified after the prior WANDS datasets, labels, model outputs, 0.5-threshold supervised results, embedding result, and local-service results have all been observed. It is not a new blind test, a registered hypothesis test, or evidence of general model superiority. Its purpose is to check the fairness of the fixed 0.5 threshold used by the existing feature-limited logistic reference. This classifier receives labeled training examples; the zero-shot LLM plans do not.

## Calibration, fixed before the new run

Use the 160 training queries and 5,120 candidates in `run_wands_160/inputs.jsonl`. Shuffle the 160 stored query indices with Python `random.Random(20271229)` and split the permutation into five consecutive blocks of 32 queries. For each fold, use one block as validation, exclude all its queries from training, and additionally remove every remaining training candidate whose product ID or exact serialized product text occurs in that fold's validation candidates. Record the excluded counts and verify query, product-ID, and exact-text disjointness between fold training and validation.

Fit exactly the existing lexical reference configuration: the `run_supervised_baseline.features` feature function, 2^18 feature-hash buckets with no alternate sign and float32 inputs, and L2 logistic regression with C=1, lbfgs, intercept, no class weights, max_iter=1000, tol=1e-4 and random_state 20271211. Fit on CPU with threadpool limit two. Record fold sizes, positive counts, iterations, convergence warnings, and out-of-fold positive probabilities for every candidate. If any fold fails or does not converge, preserve the failure and do not silently tune the procedure.

Choose one threshold using only the 5,120 out-of-fold probabilities and the original training labels. The candidate thresholds are the unique observed probabilities, in descending order; select the threshold giving the largest pooled candidate F1, breaking exact F1 ties in favor of the largest threshold. Predict positive when probability is at least the threshold. This is a score-threshold choice for candidate F1; it is not trained to minimize query-ratio MAE and should not be described as calibrated probability estimation.

## Exposed test samples and fixed analysis

Apply that threshold, without further tuning, to the already-saved full-training-model test probabilities in `run_wands_supervised_reference/predictions.jsonl`. Those probabilities were produced by the existing classifier fitted on all 160 training queries; cross-fold models are used only to choose the threshold. Check the existing full-model training and test normalized query strings, sampled product IDs, and exact serialized product texts are disjoint for each test sample.

Evaluate on the previously exposed 160-query keyed holdout, 96-query trace-derived disagreement fallback, and 52-query actual selective fallback. Compare the calibrated-threshold lexical classifier with its existing 0.5-threshold version, Qwen, and the untrained all-query-words baseline. Samples and Qwen plans differ across rows, and cannot be compared as an isolated plan treatment. Preserve adverse as well as favorable outcomes.

For each sample report candidate F1, balanced accuracy, and query-macro ratio MAE on common-complete pools. For calibrated-threshold classifier minus Qwen, compute descriptive paired query-bootstrap 95% percentile intervals for F1 and ratio MAE using 4,000 resamples and `numpy.random.default_rng` seeds 20271241, 20271242, and 20271243 respectively. No multiple-comparison correction, confirmation threshold, population coverage or generality claim is made. Product and query dependencies and reuse of already exposed data remain limitations.

Freeze this protocol, the runner and independent verifier, source features and parsers, training inputs, existing full-model probabilities and comparison outputs, and the test-selection inputs before fitting the cross-fold models. Retain fold assignment, per-candidate out-of-fold probabilities, new predictions, the chosen threshold, model iterations, summary metrics and bootstrap results. An independent verifier should reconstruct the folds, refit the fold and full-training classifiers, reparse Qwen outputs, and independently recalculate calibration, predictions, metrics and intervals.
