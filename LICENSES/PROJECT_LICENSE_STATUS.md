# Project-authored material: recorded license status

The recovered historical package explicitly did not assign an open-source
license to its newly authored scientific scripts, text, figures, or outputs.
This staging step preserves that fact rather than inventing a copyright holder
or licensing authorization. Third-party notices continue to apply separately.

Authorization to publish the repository and reviewed artifacts does not itself
specify MIT, Apache-2.0, CC BY, or another project-wide license. Until the rights
holder selects a license, this repository must not advertise itself as OSI
open source or imply a general relicensing of datasets or the manuscript.
The publication maintainer can replace this status with an authorized,
scope-specific grant while retaining all third-party notices and source hashes.
